WHAT YOU GET

More than publishing.

01

Your own public profile

Profile photo, bio, beats and social links. Visitors can click your byline and see everything you have published.

02

Build an audience

Readers can follow you without exposing follower counts. When you publish, followers can receive in-site notifications and optional email previews.

03

Use your platform

As your audience grows, you can use your PAPARAZZI presence to promote legitimate projects, services, affiliate offers or creator work — while keeping sponsored or commercial material clearly identified.

04

Claim stories from the tip desk

Anonymous tips can become leads you work on. Claim one, turn it into a draft, add reporting and publish it under your byline.

05

Publish from your workflow

The contributor API foundation is built so PAPARAZZI can connect to tools such as Telegram later, without creating a second publishing system.

06

Professional credibility

A consistent archive, profile and byline gives your writing a public home you can point people to.
