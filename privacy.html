Privacy Policy

PAPARAZZI uses account information, contributor profile information, submissions and notification preferences to operate the service.

Information we collect

Account email, display name and authentication data; public profile information you choose to publish; stories and media you submit; and notification/follow preferences.

How we use it

To authenticate you, publish your chosen profile and stories, deliver notifications, protect the service, moderate submissions and improve the newsroom.

Public information

Contributor profiles, bylines, published stories and social links you choose to display are public. Do not put private information in public profile fields.

Tips

Anonymous tips are kept out of public pages during review. Media submitted with tips may be reviewed by PAPARAZZI staff and contributors who are authorized to work on a claimed story.

Email

Transactional email may include verification codes and account messages. Optional story notifications can be controlled through notification settings.
